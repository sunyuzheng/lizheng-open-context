## 几条共同的规则

1. **注明出处**：写明作者（立正 / Yuzheng Sun；AI 整理和译稿要写明由 AI 撰写）、标题和原始链接；改过内容要说明改了。
2. **不冒充、不背书**：可以说「基于立正的公开资料」，不能说成立正本人说的、官方出品，或暗示立正认可你的产品。AI 整理和译稿不是立正的原话。
3. **以文件为准**：文件开头的 `license` 字段说了算。如果两处说法不一致，按更窄的理解，并欢迎开 issue 指出。
4. **已经发出的授权不收回**：按 CC BY 4.0 或 CC0 发布过的版本，许可一直有效；以后的版本可能调整范围。
5. **他人的内容不因收录而获得许可**：嘉宾的话、引文、链接页面、书籍版式、商标和姓名，仍归原权利人。

每一类包括哪些具体材料、为什么这样分，见 [`LICENSE-CONTENT.md`](LICENSE-CONTENT.md)。

Common rules:

1. **Attribute**: name the author (Yuzheng Sun / 立正; say when AI wrote a synthesis or translation), the title, and the original link, and say if you changed anything.
2. **No impersonation or endorsement**: you may say "based on Yuzheng Sun's public material", but not that he said it, that it is official, or that he endorses your product. AI syntheses and translations are not his words.
3. **The file decides**: a file's own `license` field governs. If two statements differ, follow the narrower one and open an issue.
4. **Grants are not withdrawn**: versions released under CC BY 4.0 or CC0 stay under those licenses; later versions may change scope.
5. **Inclusion is not a license**: guests' words, quotations, linked pages, book layouts, trademarks, and names stay with their rights holders.
