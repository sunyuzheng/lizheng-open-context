**职位描述：** 我们正在寻找一位对AI领域有热情、有网感的英文视频剪辑师。

英文频道将会专注AI前沿发展、应用、课程及行业专家访谈。剪辑风格将模仿知名创作者 Ryan Peterman 的快节奏模式——视频开头设置高光时刻和频道介绍，正片部分则要求剪辑流畅，精准去除口误和冗余信息，无需复杂的视觉特效。

**主要职责:**

- **视频剪辑:** 按照要求完成视频的初步剪辑和最终输出，确保成片节奏明快、信息清晰。
- **内容理解:** 深入理解每期视频的核心信息和风格，能够独立判断并剪掉不必要的“气口”和“废话”。
- **素材管理:** 有效管理视频素材，并能在开头部分精准挑选“高光片段”来吸引观众。并能剪出短片段，分发到Instagram、TikTok等短视频平台
- **独立工作:** 经过1-2次磨合后，能够独立完成大部分剪辑任务，并保持稳定的出品质量。

**任职要求:**

- **剪辑能力:** 拥有视频剪辑经验，熟悉至少一种主流剪辑软件，能高效协同工作。
- **对AI的兴趣:** 对人工智能领域有浓厚兴趣，能够理解相关概念并转化为有吸引力的视频内容。
- **网感与创造力:** 熟悉当前主流社交媒体的流行趋势，能为内容带来新的想法。
- **学习与合作:** 具备良好的沟通能力和快速学习能力，愿意接受反馈并持续改进。
- **地理位置 (优先):** 坐标西雅图者优先，未来有机会参与线下内容录制及其他项目合作。

**薪酬与发展:**

- **灵活的薪酬模式:** 我们提供两种合作模式：
  1. **按项目结算:** 根据行业市场标准，按单个视频项目进行结算。
  2. **效果分成:** 对于表现优异、成果显著的合作者，我们支持按视频效果进行分成。

- **长期合作机会:** 优秀的合作者将有机会转为全职运营伙伴，共同发展。

**Job Description:** We are seeking a passionate and digitally-native Video Editor to create content for our channel, which focuses on cutting-edge AI developments, applications, courses, and interviews with industry experts. The editing style will emulate the fast-paced format of creator Ryan Peterman, featuring a strong "hook" with highlights at the beginning, followed by a clean main edit that removes filler words and pauses. No complex special effects are required.

We are looking for a creative individual who can deeply understand AI content and bring fresh ideas that drive results.

**Key Responsibilities:**

- **Video Editing:** Perform initial and final edits on video content, ensuring a fast-paced, clear, and engaging final product.
- **Content Comprehension:** Demonstrate a strong understanding of AI-related topics to independently identify and remove verbal fluff and redundant content.
- **Asset Management:** Effectively manage raw footage and skillfully select "highlight" clips for the video's introduction to capture viewer attention. Cross posting snippets on Instagram and TikTok.
- **Autonomous Work:** After a brief onboarding period (1-2 projects), the ideal candidate will be able to autonomously manage and complete most editing tasks.

**Qualifications:**

- **Editing Skills:** Experience in video editing with proficiency in at least one major editing software (e.g., Final Cut Pro, Adobe Premiere Pro).
- **Interest in AI:** A strong interest in Artificial Intelligence and the ability to translate technical concepts into compelling video content.
- **Creativity & Digital Savviness:** A keen awareness of current social media trends and the ability to contribute fresh, creative ideas.
- **Collaborative & Eager to Learn:** Good communication skills, a fast learner, and receptive to feedback.
- **Location (Preferred):** Candidates based in the Seattle area are preferred due to potential opportunities for in-person collaboration on shoots and other projects.

**Compensation & Growth:**

- **Flexible Compensation Models:** We offer two partnership models:
  1. **Project-Based Pay:** Compensation at a competitive market rate for each completed video.
  2. **Performance-Based Share:** For high-performing editors who deliver exceptional results, we offer a revenue-sharing model based on video performance.

- **Long-Term Partnership:** Outstanding collaborators will have the opportunity to transition into a full-time operational partner role as the channel grows.
