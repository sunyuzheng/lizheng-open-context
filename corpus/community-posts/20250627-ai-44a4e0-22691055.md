**7/10｜Experimentation and Causal Inference Debate: **[**https://lu.ma/v2xsdb9f**](https://lu.ma/v2xsdb9f)

- Chris Khawand — Amazon Principal Economist，”the godfather of causal inference at Amazon”

- John Meakin — Meta Sr. Staff Data Scientist，”one of the most influential data scientists at Meta and changed how DS impact is tracked”

- Svet Semov — 先后任职 Amazon 与 Meta 实验平台的数据科学家，拥有独特的双重视角

现场在Bellevue，Statsig HQ；线上也有recording and insights的选项。

议程预览：[https://docs.google.com/document/d/1EL0R7crV_JntqOje28ci3xqFc6opc8j93X44YBhWqEE](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1EL0R7crV_JntqOje28ci3xqFc6opc8j93X44YBhWqEE)
