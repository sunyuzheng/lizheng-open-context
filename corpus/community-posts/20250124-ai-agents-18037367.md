## 定义

目前我所看到市面上讲的最清楚的文章来自于Anthropic：

[Building effective agents](https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents)

其中关于agents的描述是这一段：

> Agents are emerging in production as LLMs mature in key capabilities—understanding complex inputs, engaging in reasoning and planning, using tools reliably, and recovering from errors. Agents begin their work with either a command from, or interactive discussion with, the human user. Once the task is clear, agents plan and operate independently, potentially returning to the human for further information or judgement. During execution, it's crucial for the agents to gain “ground truth” from the environment at each step (such as tool call results or code execution) to assess its progress. Agents can then pause for human feedback at checkpoints or when encountering blockers. The task often terminates upon completion, but it’s also common to include stopping conditions (such as a maximum number of iterations) to maintain control.

以及这一句话总结

> They are typically just LLMs using tools based on environmental feedback in a loop.

这段描述是准确的、具体的。但是作为定义，并不够清晰。你看过了以后，很难记住。如果你没有做过AI Agents，也无法理解这些细节对应的是什么样的implementation，所以也很难去有效提高。

所以我们需要给出一个基于 第一性原理的、MECE的、能清晰描述agentic AI和非agentic AI区分的、能直接指导实践的 定义。这个定义分两部分

第一是**Agentic AI的核心特征**：

1. 调用工具
2. 多步决策
3. 自我迭代

第二是**Agentic AI系统的四个关键组件**：

1. LLM — 分别扮演planner和executor的两个角色
2. Evaluation — 对项目成功与质量的量化标准
3. Protocol — 工具描述与标准化
4. Orchestrator — 多步执行中，和人类交互的中控台

具体内容可以参考 [#第三课：Agentic AI 的三大核心特征【视频3】](https://www.superlinear.academy/c/agentic-ai/sections/451911/lessons/1685926) ， [#第二课：四大关键技术组件：如何系统构建 Agentic AI【视频6】](https://www.superlinear.academy/c/agentic-ai/sections/451913/lessons/1685938) 。这里简单介绍一下，为什么这个核心定义要有这两部分，以及为什么这样理解可以更好指导实践。

### 核心特征的意义

我们把GenAI分成Agentic AI和非agentic AI两种。Agentic AI的代表是Cursor的agent模式、Windsurf、或Devin。非agentic AI的代表是ChatGPT、Claude，或者它们的API。

如果我们把这两个系统都看作一个黑盒，似乎我们都可以向这些系统做一些输入，得到一些输出。但是输出的核心区别是，**ChatGPT的输出是模型本身的输出**，而**agentic AI的输出是模型调度工具的输出**。这里面最重要的工具自然是编程。

- **从系统能力的角度**看，一个能调用工具的AI，意味着它可以借助外部的API、插件或脚本，去做远超自身知识和记忆边界之外的操作，比如搜索互联网，或者搜索你的私有数据库，用文档方式理解其他agents的产物，等。而不会局限在模型的训练数据与alignment上
- **从实践的角度**来看，工具的调用是Agentic AI得以“落地”的前提。没有额外的外部工具或接口，AI只能停留在对给定信息的处理，无法真正介入业务流程、无法对外部系统产生影响。有了调用工具，AI就能从“回答”变成“动手”

多步决策是agentic AI能自动化，能做更多的事情，能做的更好的关键特征。原因有两点：

1. 很多任务**需要多个步骤**。比如规划市场活动，先做市场调研，再结合结果进行策略制定，最后评估实施效果并在必要时进行修订。每一步都有相应的输入与输出，且步骤间互为前后依赖。
2. 多个步骤，能有效收集更多信息、评估、提升质量。因为一个大任务，一定有一些环节做不好，拆分清楚了，才能知道问题出在哪里，如何提高。更何况，由于AI的context window（ [#研究：LLM 的内部运作机制 —— 记忆、知识与上下文窗口 / Research: How LLMs work internally - Memory, knowledge, and context window](https://www.superlinear.academy/c/ai/sections/334453/lessons/1240494) ），AI如果花越多“脑力”去记忆知识和理解任务，就只能分配更少“脑力”去执行任务，极大降低效果。

自我迭代的核心是“基于动态反馈不断修正或改进自身的方案”，这与传统一次性推理的AI形成了明显区分。这不光是“AI能debug自己”这么简单。回到第一点，agentic AI需要与环境产生输入输出的双向交互，在输出改变了环境后，能通过输入去改变自己的策略，从而产出更好的输出，这样的系统才是一个有效的、稳健的系统。

This year, however, the ecosystem surrounding AI agents has finally started to mature. On one hand, this rapid progress has led to a faster consensus in how people understand and approach AI agents. On the other hand, it has attracted increased investment and talent to the field. As a result, we’re seeing the accelerated development of AI agents. Based on our projections, starting in early 2025, AI agents will open a 10-to-18-month window of opportunity for early adopters.

There are many important topics to discuss around agentic AI. But for this article, we’ll focus on one fundamental question: **What is agentic AI?**

### What Is Agentic AI?

You may have already noticed that AI agents can “get things done.” Today, they can replace programmers; tomorrow, they might replace salespeople. But what makes agentic AI different from “regular AI”?

- Why can it perform tasks?
- What kinds of tasks can it handle?
- What tasks should we allow it to perform?

Other practical questions include:

- Is LangChain an example of agentic AI? What about ChatGPT operators?
- How do they differ, and which approach holds more potential for the future?

And perhaps the most important question:

- What is the ceiling for agentic AI? Where does the technology stand today, and how can we gradually improve its quality?

Additionally, if you want to build your own agentic AI system, what tools should you use to achieve your goals?

### A Useful Definition of Agentic AI

One of the clearest descriptions of AI agents I’ve seen comes from Anthropic. They define AI agents like this:

> Agents are emerging in production as LLMs mature in key capabilities—understanding complex inputs, engaging in reasoning and planning, using tools reliably, and recovering from errors. Agents begin their work with either a command from, or interactive discussion with, the human user. Once the task is clear, agents plan and operate independently, potentially returning to the human for further information or judgement. During execution, it's crucial for the agents to gain “ground truth” from the environment at each step (such as tool call results or code execution) to assess its progress. Agents can then pause for human feedback at checkpoints or when encountering blockers. The task often terminates upon completion, but it’s also common to include stopping conditions (such as a maximum number of iterations) to maintain control.

Anthropic further summarizes this by stating:

> They are typically just LLMs using tools based on environmental feedback in a loop.

This description is accurate and detailed, but it’s not particularly memorable as a definition. If you’re not already familiar with AI agents, it can be hard to connect these details to real-world implementations. And without a clear framework, it’s difficult to know how to improve such systems effectively.

So, we need a definition grounded in **first principles**—a MECE (mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive) framework that clearly distinguishes agentic AI from non-agentic AI and serves as a practical guide for implementation.

Our definition has two parts:

1. **The Core Features of Agentic AI:**
  - Tool usage
  - Multi-step decision-making
  - Self-iteration

2. **The Four Key Components of Agentic AI Systems:**
  - **LLM:** Functions as both the planner and the executor.
  - **Evaluation:** Establishes quantifiable standards for success and quality.
  - **Protocol:** Provides tool descriptions and standardization.
  - **Orchestrator:** Serves as the central console for managing multi-step tasks and human interactions.

### Why These Core Features Matter

We can divide generative AI into two categories: **agentic AI** and **non-agentic AI**. Examples of agentic AI include Cursor’s agent mode, Windsurf, or Devin. Examples of non-agentic AI include ChatGPT, Claude, or their APIs.

If we treat both types as black boxes, it might seem like we’re just giving inputs and receiving outputs from each system. However, the key difference lies in the **source of the output**:

- Non-agentic AI outputs are generated solely by the model itself.
- Agentic AI outputs, by contrast, are generated by the model **leveraging external tools**.

The most important tool, of course, is programming.

From a capabilities perspective, an AI that can use tools has access to resources far beyond its own training data and memory. It can perform tasks like searching the web, querying private databases, interpreting outputs from other agents, and more. This enables it to go beyond its own knowledge and alignment constraints.

From a practical standpoint, **tool usage is what allows agentic AI to “get its hands dirty”** in real-world tasks. Without external tools or interfaces, AI is limited to processing the information it’s given. It can’t engage with external systems or workflows in a meaningful way. Tool usage transforms AI from being a passive "answer machine" to becoming an active "doer."

Why Multi-Step Decision-Making Is Key

Multi-step decision-making enables agentic AI to:

1. **Handle complex tasks:**
Many tasks require several steps. For instance, running a marketing campaign might involve conducting market research, creating a strategy based on the findings, and evaluating results to make adjustments. Each step has its own inputs, outputs, and dependencies.
2. **Improve task quality:**
Breaking a big task into smaller steps allows the system to identify and fix specific weak points, improving overall performance. Additionally, due to LLMs’ limited context windows, the more effort an AI spends on retaining information, the less “brainpower” it can dedicate to actually executing tasks. Multi-step workflows mitigate this limitation by dividing cognitive load.

The Role of Self-Iteration

At its core, self-iteration means **“adapting dynamically based on feedback”**—a clear departure from traditional one-shot AI reasoning.

Self-iteration isn’t just about debugging. It’s about enabling the agent to interact with its environment, assess the impact of its outputs, and adjust its strategies accordingly. This feedback loop is what makes agentic AI systems robust and effective in dynamic settings.

### Why These Components Matter

The four key components—LLM, evaluation, protocol, and orchestrator—focus on how to **actually build agentic AI systems**. This will be explored in future articles. For now, I hope this introduction helps you cut through the noise and pinpoint the key concepts when navigating the fast-evolving world of agentic AI. This clarity will be especially critical over the next year.
