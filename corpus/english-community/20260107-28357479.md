# Community Introduction

*Author: **课代表立正** | **[See original](https://www.superlinear.academy/c/ai-resources/community-introduction)** | **Reposted by Superlinear Bot*

---

"GenAI Builders" is a membership community with three types of contributors:

1. Former Maven course students;
2. Current students for [#02 · AI Builders · 可靠交付](https://www.superlinear.academy/c/ai) ;
3. Specially invited AI industry leaders.

Our community has several "spaces":

- [#咖啡馆](https://www.superlinear.academy/c/general-discussions) : For industry news, new tricks, inspirations, and thoughts.
- [#Share Your Projects](https://www.superlinear.academy/c/share-your-projects) : Share your projects or articles. We encourage sharing even undeveloped ideas for valuable feedback.
- [#Q&A](https://www.superlinear.academy/c/q-a) : Dedicated to queries. Detailed questions get more useful answers.
- [#02 · AI Builders · 可靠交付](https://www.superlinear.academy/c/ai) : Self-paced version of our AI productivity course, also the community entry point. Check out the course and community insights here.

New members can:

- Introduce themselves in the [#自我介绍](https://www.superlinear.academy/c/say-hello) . It’s public and shareable across apps. Introductions help build connections. I read and respond to each one.
- Ask questions in the [#Q&A](https://www.superlinear.academy/c/q-a) and share ideas in the [#Share Your Projects](https://www.superlinear.academy/c/share-your-projects) . If it gains interest, compile and post it.
- Check out our course! It’s the most up-to-date version.
